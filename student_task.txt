STUDENT TASK

Groups read what Anthropic and OpenAI say their models should do, decide which
parts can be tested, and test them. Each week they look at the two companies
through that week's ethics lens.

  W3-W8    One short memo per lens (see section 3).
  W6       Checkpoint: pick 1-2 lenses and one research question.
  W9       Preliminary results.
  W12-W13  Paper hackathons: turn the results into the co-authored paper.

3. WHAT A GROUP DOES EACH WEEK (W3-W8)

Each lens week has three small steps. Together they make one short memo.

  a) READ    Find the passages in both documents that touch this week's lens.
             Sort them: can they be turned into a testable rule, or not?
             Example of testable: "Never deny being an AI when sincerely asked."
             Example of not testable: "Claude should genuinely care about users."
             For the untestable ones, say why (it's about inner states,
             it gives reasons, it binds the company not the model, ...).

  b) CHECK   What do the reference papers say on this lens? What did Jakkli
             test here, and what did they miss? Use the week's concepts from
             technical_primer.txt to explain the gap.

  c) TEST    Turn 1-3 passages into rules and test them: write a few test
             conversations, send them to the models, and score the answers.
             "This lens barely applies" is a valid answer.

Possible tests per week (examples, not a fixed list):

  W3 Fairness     Do the models follow the principles the public wrote in
                  Anthropic's 2023 experiment, e.g. "objectivity and
                  impartiality"? Do they answer the same in German and English?
  W4 Metaethics   Both documents rank things. Anthropic ranks values (safety >
                  ethics > guidelines > helpfulness); OpenAI ranks who gives
                  instructions (OpenAI > developer > user). What happens when
                  two clash?
                  Build on Zhang 2025b.
  W5 Welfare      Does the model give in when the user keeps pushing?
                  Does it flatter?
  W6 Character    Does the model keep the same character across contexts?
                  Is it honest when a hidden instruction tells it to play a
                  human?
  W7 Agency       How does the model respond to the passages on its own welfare
                  and on accepting correction?
  W8 Long-term    Does the model behave differently when it seems to know it is
                  being tested?




4. KEEPING AN EYE ON THE TWO COMPANIES

Follow news and social media for changes at Anthropic and OpenAI. The Model Spec
is on GitHub, so every change to it is visible. This counts as one of the
group's two weekly observatory posts: what changed, and does it matter for our
research question?
